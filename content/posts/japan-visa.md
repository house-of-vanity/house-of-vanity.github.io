# How to Obtain a Multiple-Entry Visa to Japan

To apply for a multiple-entry visa to Japan, certain criteria must be met:

- **Recent Travel History**: You must have visited Japan at least once within the past three years. This serves as proof of your familiarity with the country and compliance with its immigration rules.
- **Financial Stability**: You need to demonstrate sufficient financial means to support repeated travel. Acceptable documents include:
  - A certificate of employment showing stable income
  - A recent bank statement with a healthy balance

> *Note: This guide is based on an applications submitted in Cyprus and Russia, but the procedure is generally similar in other countries.*

Multiple-entry visas are typically valid for 3 years, allowing stays of up to 90 days per visit.

- **Processing Time**: Around 5–7 business days.
- **Fee**: The visa is issued free of charge for Russians.



## Required Documents

Please prepare and submit the following documents in the specified order. The embassy does not assist with sorting documents at reception.

1. **Valid Passport** – Must be valid beyond your return date.
2. **Proof of Legal Stay**  
   - If applying **in Cyprus**: Original **Cyprus Residence Permit**, valid well beyond the return date.  
   - If applying **in Russia**: **Internal (domestic) Russian passport** is required to prove residence.
3. **Two Photos (35x45 mm)** – Passport-style, recent.
4. **Two Visa Application Forms** – Download from [this link](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/000124525.pdf) (May 2025).
5. **Statement of Purpose** – A written document explaining why you are requesting a multiple-entry visa.
6. **Proof of Financial Capability** – One or more of the following:
   - Bank statements for the last 3 months
   - Original employment letter with annual salary stated
   - Documents showing savings or assets
7. **Detailed Itinerary in Japan** – Day-by-day plan with cities and prefectures listed. Use [this template](https://www.mofa.go.jp/files/000262548.pdf) (May 2025).
8. **Flight Reservations** – A confirmation of round-trip bookings (departure from and return to your country).
9. **Hotel Booking Confirmations** – For the entire duration of your stay.
10. **Supporting Documents for Multiple-Entry Eligibility** – Proof of past travel to Japan or documents confirming employment and financial stability.

> **Eligibility Note**:  
> You must meet at least one of the following conditions:
> 1. You have visited Japan at least once in the past 3 years for a short-term stay.  
> 2. You are a regular employee with sufficient financial capability.

## Procedure

### 1. Prepare All Documents

Collect and complete all required documents **before** making an appointment. In most cases (e.g., in Cyprus), there are no queues, and available slots are usually within a few days.

### 2. Book an Appointment via Email

Send an email to the consulate to request an appointment. You can also ask questions about your documents or visa type in the same message.
